About

A bit more about me beyond the resume.

I'm a senior at MIT studying AI + Decision Making and Mathematics, with a focus on statistical inference and applied data science.

My work spans NLP for political-economy research, deep-learning models for clinical time-series, and game-theoretic analysis of network voting. On campus I serve as president of SAAS (the South Asian Association of Students) and MITeri, MIT's Nepali community.

Outside of coursework I speak Nepali, English, and Spanish.

  • Based in Cambridge, MA
  • Languages Nepali · English · Spanish
